DPI (dots per inch) sets how many pixels are printed per inch of physical paper. 300 DPI is the standard target for sharp photographic prints at normal viewing distance; large-format prints viewed from further away (posters, banners) can use lower DPI (100–150) since individual pixels are less visible at distance.
